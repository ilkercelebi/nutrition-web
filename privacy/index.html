Nutriva — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026 · Last updated: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how İlker Çelebi ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Nutriva mobile application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account: your email address and display name, and a user ID, when you sign up with email/password, Google, or Apple. (Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.)
  • Health & profile data: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, weight goal, dietary preference, and any allergies or foods to avoid you enter. This is used to calculate your targets and personalize guidance.
  • Nutrition activity: food log entries (names, quantities, calories and macronutrients), weight entries, daily goals, and logging streaks.
  • Photos: images you capture or upload for AI food analysis. They are processed to estimate nutrition and may be stored in your account.
  • Social features (optional): your referral code, friend connections, and comments you post. If you turn on “share my diary,” friends you accept can view your food diary and comment on it. This is off by default.
  • Coach messages: the questions you send to the in-app AI coach.

Information collected automatically

  • Purchases: subscription status and transaction identifiers (via RevenueCat and the app stores). We never receive your full card number.
  • Usage & diagnostics: app analytics events (Firebase Analytics), crash reports and device diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics), and a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Advertising: for free users we show ads via Google AdMob, which may access an advertising identifier and ad-interaction data. On iOS we ask for tracking permission first (App Tracking Transparency).

2. How we use your information

  • Provide the App: calculate calorie/macro targets, log meals, track weight.
  • Analyze food photos and answer your AI-coach questions.
  • Personalize guidance and respect your dietary preferences and allergies.
  • Manage subscriptions and unlock premium features.
  • Send reminders and notifications you enable.
  • Show and measure ads for free users.
  • Protect the service against abuse (Firebase App Check) and fix crashes.

3. AI processing

To estimate nutrition from photos and to power the coach and recipe features, the relevant content (food images, coach messages, recipe text) is sent through our secure server to the Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service (GPT-4o mini). Azure OpenAI processes this content to generate a response and, per Microsoft's terms, does not use it to train the underlying models. AI outputs are estimates and are not medical, dietary, or health advice.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the App:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), photo storage, analytics, crash reporting, messaging, remote config, App Check.
  • Microsoft Azure OpenAI — AI analysis of photos and text.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Google AdMob — advertising for free users.
  • Apple / Google — sign-in and payment processing.
  • Other friends in the App — only the diary/comments you choose to share.

We may also disclose information to comply with law or protect our rights.

5. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App (Profile → Account → Delete), which permanently removes your profile, logs, and photos from our systems. You may also email us to request deletion.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at destek@nutriva.app.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Sensitive fields (like your subscription status and scan quotas) are writable only by our server, and App Check helps block abusive clients. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International transfers

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate (for example, Firebase and Azure OpenAI data centers). These countries may have different data-protection laws than yours.

9. Children

Nutriva is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum digital-consent age in your country, e.g. 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date, and where required we will notify you in the App.
