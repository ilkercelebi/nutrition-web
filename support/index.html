Frequently asked questions

How does the AI food scan work?

Take or pick a photo of your meal and Nutriva estimates the food, portion, calories, and macros. Estimates are approximate — you can edit any value before saving.

How accurate are the numbers?

They're AI estimates meant to guide you, not exact measurements. For anything health-critical, verify ingredients yourself and consult a professional.

What's included in Premium?

More daily AI scans, the AI coach, an ad-free experience, and cook-mode/insights. You can subscribe from the paywall in the app.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Restore: open the paywall and tap “Restore purchases.” Cancel: manage it in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings — subscriptions are billed by the store, not by us.

How do allergies and the coach work together?

Add any allergies or foods to avoid during onboarding or in Profile → Plan. The AI coach treats them as a hard rule and won't suggest them. It's a convenience, not a safety guarantee — always check ingredients yourself.

How do I add friends?

Profile → Friends. Share your code or enter a friend's. If you turn on “share my diary,” accepted friends can see your day and cheer you on. It's off by default.

Notifications aren't showing up

Make sure notifications are allowed for Nutriva in your device Settings, and that reminders are enabled in Profile. Then set your reminder time in the app.

How do I delete my account and data?

Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, logs, and photos. You can also email us to request deletion.

Which languages are supported?

Nutriva is available in 14 languages and follows your device language.
